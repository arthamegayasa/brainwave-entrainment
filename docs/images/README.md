# README visuals

These files document SwaraSanti without changing the app.

- `swarasanti-banner.svg`: original imagined dawn over still water in the app's ink, teal, and warm-light palette. Decorative, not a recording or EEG trace.
- `session-curve.svg`: original explanatory diagram derived from `src/audio/presets.ts` and `src/audio/schedule.ts`. It illustrates the 30-minute Meditating audio schedule: `(0, 10)`, `(12, 6)`, `(25, 6)`, `(30, 10)` in minutes and Hz. It is not a recording or a prediction of brain activity.
- `swarasanti-home.jpg`: actual local landing screen with its painted night-lake hero.
- `swarasanti-sessions.jpg`: actual local session-selection screen showing the eight painted goal scenes.
- `swarasanti-session-setup.jpg`: actual local Meditating session setup dialog before playback.

Screenshots were captured from the local application with `npm run dev` in a standalone, signed-out browser session on 2026-09-26. Motion was disabled during capture so every frame is stable. No patient information or fabricated application controls are shown. Images use relative paths so the README works in forks and offline checkouts.

## In-app scene paintings

`public/scenes/` holds eighteen original paintings created with GPT Image 2.5 Flare (`openrouter/openai/gpt-image-2.5-flare`) through the omp image role. Ten were created on 2026-09-26: the landing hero, one scene per goal, and the session-complete sunrise, which was edited from the hero so the journey ends at the same lake. Each painting ships as WebP at 768 px (cards; precached for offline use) and 1536 px (hero, banner, player, and completion; cached on first view).

Three Nature Scenes for Custom Audio were created on 2026-09-27 at 1536×1024, in the same luminous matte-painting style (soft painterly brushwork, glowing light, atmospheric haze; no people, animals, text, logos, or borders). The owner picked one of three variants per Scene on [#44](https://github.com/arthamegayasa/brainwave-entrainment/issues/44):

- `deep-ocean` (Deep Ocean): a calm deep ocean seen from underwater, a soft sun glowing through the rippling surface at the upper right, slanting light shafts into turquoise water that deepens to navy, faint rock arches and a low reef ridge in haze near the seabed, and tiny floating motes.
- `nebula` (Nebula): a softly glowing nebula in deep space, billowing rose, violet, and teal clouds flowing across the lower half, a bright warm star just above centre, and fine stars across the dark indigo sky.
- `lavender-field` (Lavender Field): a lavender hillside at dawn with one soft-edged tree on the crest, the sun rising behind thin clouds at the upper right and sending gentle shafts across the flowers, low mist in the valley beyond, and glowing pollen above the blossoms.

Their live layers use only effects the other Scenes already have: light shafts falling from the surface, floating motes, and mist over the seabed (Deep Ocean); twinkling stars, a meteor, and the star's bloom (Nebula); floating pollen, light shafts from the sun, and low mist in the valley (Lavender Field). Like every Scene, each also has the pulsing bloom on its light and one drifting mist band, which on Nebula runs through the brightest clouds.

Five Science Scenes for Custom Audio were created on 2026-09-27 at 1536×1024, as calm, non-clinical scientific illustrations in the same style: luminous matte painting, soft painterly brushwork, glowing light and atmospheric haze, a dark ink-teal ground with warm light and soft depth. Every prompt, and the review of every variant, kept to the avoid-list of [#45](https://github.com/arthamegayasa/brainwave-entrainment/issues/45): no realistic organs or tissue, blood, skulls, hospitals, medical equipment (EEG caps, electrodes, needles, scanners), DNA helices, text, numbers, charts, logos, borders, or human faces. The model drew facial profiles for a head silhouette, so the Sound to Mind prompts turned the head away from the viewer. The owner picked one of three variants per Scene on [#45](https://github.com/arthamegayasa/brainwave-entrainment/issues/45#issuecomment-5853433457):

- `neuron-grove` (Neuron Grove, variant B): a grove of tall trees shaped like neurons, each with a glowing round cell body and branching dendrites that interlace overhead into a canopy of fine gold light, the brightest cell body at the upper right, a soft path between the trunks, teal haze, and floating golden motes.
- `synapse-light` (Synapse Light, variant C): a quiet web of fine teal filaments with small glowing endings, two of them meeting across a tiny gap that shines with warm light at the centre, and specks of golden light floating like fireflies.
- `mind-constellation` (Mind Constellation, variant C): a sparse constellation of stars joined by delicate threads of light that traces the soft outline of a brain seen from the side, one warm bright star within it, above a sea of moonlit clouds with a faint warm glow on the horizon.
- `sound-to-mind` (Sound to Mind, variant B): rings of golden light rippling out from a radiant glow low on the horizon across a misty plain at dusk, like sound travelling through still air, toward the featureless silhouette of a seated figure seen from behind in the foreground, with floating golden motes.
- `brainwave-ribbons` (Brainwave Ribbons, variant A): long translucent ribbons of violet, teal, and gold light shaped like gentle waves, crossing a calm dark space and meeting in a warm glow at the centre, with fine stars above.

Their live layers use only effects the other Scenes already have: floating motes among the trunks and mist over the grove's floor (Neuron Grove); floating motes around the gap (Synapse Light); twinkling stars, a meteor, and the bright star's bloom (Mind Constellation); light rays bursting from the glow on the horizon and floating motes over the plain (Sound to Mind); aurora curtains over the ribbons and twinkling stars (Brainwave Ribbons). Like every Scene, each also has the pulsing bloom on its light and one drifting mist band. Brainwave Ribbons is the default Scene for Custom Audio without a chosen one.

`src/ui/scenes.ts` records where each painting's light source, water, mist, and particles sit, and which Scenes Clinicians and the Admin can pick for Custom Audio: under Nature, the eight goal paintings labelled by what they show rather than a result (Moon over Clouds, Misty Peak, Sunset Shore, Forest Light, Dawn Hills, Desert Dunes, Aurora Lake, Summer Meadow), then Deep Ocean, Nebula, and Lavender Field; under Science, Neuron Grove, Synapse Light, Mind Constellation, Sound to Mind, and Brainwave Ribbons; Custom Audio without a pick shows Brainwave Ribbons. `src/ui/SceneArt.tsx` crops each painting around its light source to fit the frame and pins live layers to the painting's own pixel coordinates: glow, water glints and ripples, light shafts, mist, stars, aurora, and particles. Every layer is a plain HTML element animated only through `transform` and `opacity`, so the GPU compositor runs all motion without main-thread paint; there are no blend modes, filters, or backdrop blur over moving content. Planes shift by depth under a mouse pointer only, since touch input scrolls the page. Animations pause offscreen, while a session is paused, and behind open dialogs; with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, only gentle opacity changes remain. The scenes are imagined places and calm illustrations, not anatomy, EEG traces, measured brain activity, or evidence of treatment effects. `public/icon.svg` renders the 192 px and 512 px PWA icons. Audio is synthesized separately in the browser. First-party design references and interpretation: [premium visual research](premium-visual-research.md).
